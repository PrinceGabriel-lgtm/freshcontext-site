Free Help-centre Snapshot

Find out what your help centre is telling your bot.

Send us your public help-centre URL. We read the public pages, check what we find by hand, and send you up to 10 issues with the links to see each one yourself.

  • Broken links, and links that now land on the home page
  • Retired pages that are still being linked to
  • Price or version contradictions, where two pages state them plainly
  • Free, with no obligation. Public pages only.

An honest result, not a sales result. A clean Snapshot is possible, and we'll tell you if your help centre builds its pages in a way our reader can't see yet. A public-page issue doesn't prove your bot repeated it; that is what the audit checks.

What happens next

Three steps, all on our side.

  1. 1

    We read your public pages

    At most 150 pages, one per second, obeying your robots.txt. If your site refuses our reader, we stop.

  2. 2

    A person checks every finding

    We open every page and delete anything we can't confirm ourselves.

  3. 3

    You get it by email

    Up to 10 findings with their links, or an honest note that we found nothing worth your time.
