Commencement gate

Application is not commencement.

We keep the commercial boundary explicit so neither side has to infer when work, payment or acceptance obligations begin.

Apply

You describe the workflow, failure mode, stack, target outcome and practical constraints.

Qualify

FreshContext confirms fit, dependencies, scope and whether the proposed engagement is worth doing.

Contract

If accepted, we issue a service order or statement of work with the controlling scope, price, acceptance criteria, IP treatment and timetable.

Invoice

The commercial pack includes an invoice and payment reference. A typical engagement uses a 50% commencement deposit unless the signed service order says otherwise.

Start

Work starts only after the required agreement is signed and the commencement deposit is verified as cleared funds.

Accept

Completion is tested against the agreed acceptance criteria before the engagement is closed and any final balance becomes due.

Open-source boundary

The MIT release and the paid engagement are different assets.

The historical public implementation remains available under its applicable MIT licence. A commercial engagement pays for the scoped work and rights actually stated in the signed service order.

Background layer

Pre-existing FreshContext software, specification expression, methods, know-how, infrastructure and other background material are identified separately from client-specific deliverables.

Client engagement

Implementation, configuration, private adaptations, deployment, operating evidence, documentation, support and any client-specific deliverables are governed by the signed commercial documents.

Boundary: an application does not grant exclusivity, assign FreshContext ownership, reserve capacity, or create an obligation to begin work. Any such right must be written into an executed agreement. Acquisition and strategic transaction enquiries are handled separately through corpdev@freshcontext.dev, not through this application flow.
