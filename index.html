The problem

Relevant is not the same as ready to rely on.

A retrieval result can be semantically relevant and still belong to the wrong product, plan, version, geography, time period or source state. FreshContext evaluates that boundary explicitly.

01

Context changes

Prices, plans, policies, releases and source pages move while stored answers and memories remain.

02

Scope matters

Fresh information can still be wrong for the exact entity, version, region, plan or task being handled.

03

Evidence matters

A decision should preserve what was observed, when it was read, what it supported and why it was accepted, held or escalated.

Claim discipline

We keep evidence, uncertainty and capability boundaries explicit.

FreshContext is designed to support reviewable decisions, not to manufacture certainty.

Unresolved stays unresolved

If the available evidence cannot settle a claim, FreshContext does not quietly convert uncertainty into approval.

Applicability is explicit

Identity, version, time, source role and workflow policy can matter independently of semantic similarity.

Evidence is preserved

Where supported, the decision record retains the observation, timing, scope and reasoning needed for later review or replay.

Human review remains visible

The current commercial assessment is human-reviewed. We do not present it as a fully autonomous operating service.

Signatures do not create truth

A signed record can protect integrity and attribution; it does not make the original source factually correct.

Deployment claims stay bounded

Assessment, integration and production acceptance are separate scopes with separate evidence and obligations.

Where it fits

Between information arriving and the next system relying on it.

FreshContext is designed as a reusable context-integrity layer that can be integrated into different AI, retrieval, agent and human-review workflows through bounded interfaces and workflow-specific configuration.

It does not replace vector databases, agent frameworks, help desks, model guardrails or governance systems. It addresses the narrower question of whether supported context is appropriate to rely on at a defined decision point.
