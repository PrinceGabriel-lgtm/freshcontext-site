Why answers go stale

The bot didn't change. The pages under it did.

Support bots answer from help-centre articles, docs and policy pages. Nobody tells the bot when those pages change, and nobody tells you which answers the change broke.

01

Prices and limits move

A plan goes from $49 to $59, a free tier drops from 5 seats to 3. The old number lives on in answers.

02

Plans and versions are renamed

“Pro” becomes “Growth”, v2 is retired. The bot still recommends what no longer exists.

03

Pages retire and links die

Answers point customers at pages that now 404, or quietly redirect to the home page.

    How monitoring works

Rules we don't bend

An answer we can't settle is never called fine.

An audit is only useful if you can trust its “all clear”. So the method is built to say “we couldn't tell” rather than guess.

Unclear stays unclear

If the source can't settle a claim, the report says so. It is never counted as current.

A person checks every finding

Nothing reaches you that we haven't opened and checked against the page ourselves.

Every read is logged

Each finding carries the page, the passage and the time we read it, so you can check it yourself.

No AI judge unless you allow it

Checks are rule-based. An optional Anthropic-only AI step for unclear answers stays off unless your signed Order allows it.

Public pages, your permission

The Snapshot reads only public pages and respects robots.txt. We never query your bot without permission.

Independent of your vendor

We don't sell the bot, so we have no reason to grade it kindly.

Why not your help desk's own tool?

They check the articles. We check the answers.

Intercom's Fin and Zendesk's knowledge tools now flag outdated or conflicting articles inside their own knowledge base. That is useful, and we recommend it.

We check something different: what your bot actually told customers, against the live sources it relied on, including pricing pages, docs and policies outside the help centre. And we are independent of the vendor whose bot is being checked.
