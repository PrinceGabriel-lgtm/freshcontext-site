Strategic technology enquiries

A defined technology transaction, not a fundraising round.

FreshContext is working, founder-owned, pre-revenue software focused on assessing whether context is appropriate to rely on in AI-assisted workflows. The founder is open to qualified discussions regarding a defined acquisition or strategic licence of proprietary technology and associated rights.

This is an enquiry route for prospective technology buyers. Commercial customers seeking a paid workflow assessment should use services and pricing.

Transaction overview

The capability under consideration

FreshContext applies explicit source, identity, time, applicability and provenance boundaries to information entering AI-assisted decisions. It supports review of stale, mismatched, conflicting or insufficiently attributable context, with traceable evidence and review outcomes.

This is a potential transaction in defined software/IP assets and associated know-how, not an offer to sell an established recurring-revenue company. Scope, transferable rights, exclusions, historical public-source licence permissions and transition obligations would be established through diligence and written agreement.

Potential strategic fit

Enterprise AI infrastructure, developer platforms, data governance, retrieval and workflow products where source applicability and decision traceability are material.

Possible transaction routes

Defined asset acquisition or appropriately scoped strategic licensing, subject to commercial fit, rights verification and legal agreement.

Commercial services remain separate

Paid Context Integrity Assessments and integrations are service engagements, not transfers of FreshContext ownership or source-code rights.

Initial qualification

Start with a non-confidential enquiry.

For an initial discussion, email immanuel@freshcontext.dev with the subject FreshContext - Strategic Technology Enquiry.

Please identify the organisation, relevant decision-maker, acquisition or licensing interest, intended strategic application, confirmation that pre-revenue software/IP assets are in scope, and the anticipated review process.

Do not send confidential information, credentials, sensitive personal data or proprietary documents in the initial enquiry. Technical materials, repository access, confidential financial information and diligence are handled only after appropriate counterparty qualification and agreed protections.

No initial enquiry creates an exclusivity arrangement, an option, an obligation to negotiate, an assignment of rights or a binding transaction. Any deal is subject to separate written terms and applicable legal requirements.
